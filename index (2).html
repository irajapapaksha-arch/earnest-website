How to start freelancing as a beginner

Understand what freelancing is and how to prepare before looking for your first client.

Beginner

1. Choose one service

Start with a skill you can practise, such as proofreading, simple graphic design, short-video editing, or document formatting. Avoid claiming skills you do not have.

2. Make 2–3 samples

Create sample projects to demonstrate your work. Clearly label practice examples as samples, not paid client work.

3. Research platforms carefully

Read the platform's fees, minimum age, country eligibility, payment methods and dispute rules before creating an account.

  • Use a clear and honest profile.
  • Agree on scope and delivery dates before starting.
  • Keep messages and payments within the platform when its rules require it.
Safety tip: a real client should not need your password, one-time verification code, or an upfront payment to “release” your earnings.
🎨CREATIVE SKILLS
Design · 3 min read

Build a simple design portfolio with free tools

Plan a small set of sample designs that can show potential clients what you can do.

Practice project

Make a focused collection

Choose one theme, such as café promotions, event flyers, YouTube thumbnails or social media posts. Create a few consistent samples rather than lots of unrelated designs.

Show your process

  • Use readable typography and strong contrast.
  • Check spelling, alignment and mobile readability.
  • Use assets that you created or are licensed to use.
  • Do not use a real brand's logo in a way that implies endorsement.

Present your work

Place your best samples in a simple portfolio page or PDF. Explain the purpose of each design and the tools used. Keep editable source files organised.

Free tools may have different asset licences. Check the licence before using a graphic commercially or reselling a template.
🤖AI TOOLS
Technology · 4 min read

Use AI tools to work smarter, not blindly

Learn where AI can help with planning and drafts, and where your own judgement matters.

All levels

Good uses for AI

  • Brainstorm ideas and organise an outline.
  • Draft a checklist or first version of a description.
  • Practise a skill with examples and feedback.
  • Summarise material you are allowed to use.

Always review the result

Check facts, dates, calculations, spelling and whether the output fits the brief. Do not submit unverified claims as facts or pass off someone else's work as your own.

Protect private information

Do not paste passwords, payment details, private client files or sensitive personal data into a tool unless you have a clear, appropriate basis and understand its data settings.

AI can support your work, but it does not guarantee clients, views or income. Check the terms of each tool and marketplace.
🎬CONTENT
Video editing · 4 min read

Learn short-video editing the responsible way

Build editing skills while respecting copyright and making genuinely original work.

Practice project

Practise with material you can use

Record your own clips or use footage with a licence that permits your intended use. Keep a note of the source and licence for every asset.

Make the edit meaningful

  • Develop a clear story or explanation.
  • Add your own narration, analysis or educational context where appropriate.
  • Use music and sound effects that are cleared for the platform and intended use.
  • Check the platform's monetisation and reused-content policies.

Adding captions, cropping or changing speed alone does not necessarily give you permission to reuse someone else's video or prevent copyright claims.

When in doubt, ask permission or choose different footage that you are allowed to use.

How to spot suspicious online earning offers

Recognise warning signs before spending money or sharing personal information.

Important

Pause if an offer:

  • Promises guaranteed or unusually high income for little work.
  • Requires you to pay to unlock a job, withdraw wages or receive a prize.
  • Pressures you to recruit people or deposit money to receive commissions.
  • Requests passwords, one-time codes or remote access to your device.
  • Uses an unofficial email address or a link that imitates a known service.

Verify independently

Visit the official website by typing its address yourself, read independent information, check payment terms and ask a trusted adult for help if you are unsure. Never send money because a stranger says your account will be blocked.

If you have already shared payment or login information, contact the relevant bank or service through its official contact details and secure your account.
🌱GROW A SITE
Website growth · 4 min read

Prepare a website before monetising it

Build useful content and trust before applying to advertising or affiliate programmes.

Website owners

Build a useful foundation

  • Publish original articles that answer real reader questions.
  • Make navigation, mobile layout and contact information clear.
  • Add an About page, privacy information and relevant disclaimers.
  • Check every link and remove misleading or broken offers.

Apply only when ready

Advertising and affiliate networks have their own eligibility rules, country availability, age requirements and content policies. Review the current official terms before applying.

Be transparent

Clearly disclose affiliate relationships near relevant links. Never fabricate reviews, visitor numbers, testimonials or earning claims.

Traffic and monetisation take time and are not guaranteed. Do not buy fake visits or click your own ads.
